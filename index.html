1National / Federal (US)

Multi-state trafficking pipeline dismantled from Detroit to West Virginia

8 lb meth · 2 lb fentanylDetroit, MI → Morgantown, WV28 charged

28 defendants were charged in a 114-count indictment over a corridor running from Detroit to Morgantown, WV, with distribution across Monongalia County and neighboring states. Agents seized over 8 lbs of methamphetamine, 2 lbs of fentanyl mixtures, a half-pound of cocaine and crack, 10 firearms and $21,906 in cash. Alleged leader Brendan Bragg, 32, of Detroit.

SourceDEA

Ten sentenced in South Florida cocaine conspiracy

5+ kg cocaineSouth Florida10 sentenced, 3 at large

Ten defendants received sentences from 21 to 188 months in a Miami-area conspiracy involving five or more kilograms of cocaine, running September 2024 through September 2025. Investigators seized more than $450,000 in drug proceeds; three co-defendants remain fugitives.

SourceDEA

CBP finds 1,675 lbs of meth in a celery shipment at Pharr, Texas

1,675 lb meth ($14.9M)Pharr International Bridge, TX

Officers at the Pharr International Bridge pulled roughly $14.9 million in methamphetamine from a commercial load of fresh celery arriving from Mexico — one of the larger single-load meth interceptions on the South Texas border this year.

SourceCBP

VA nurse indicted for diverting opioids

A Department of Veterans Affairs nurse was indicted on charges of stealing opioid medication from a VA facility — a smaller case, but part of DEA's continuing diversion-control enforcement push.

SourceDEA

3South America

Ecuador — 1,011 kg of cocaine found inside lifting machinery at Posorja

1,011 kg cocainePort of Posorja, Guayas, EcuadorNone reported

National Police drilled into the structures of four electromagnetic lifting machines in a container at the Port of Posorja, Guayas province, and found 1,011.52 kg of cocaine, about 9.23 million doses, valued between $27.7M and $43.2M. The container was declared as industrial equipment and bound for Lázaro Cárdenas, Mexico. No arrests reported.

SourceInfobae

Ecuador — Interpol red-notice trafficker captured in Guayaquil

Guayaquil, Ecuador3 across 3 countries

Rubén Bermúdez Loor, 61, wanted by US authorities on conspiracy to distribute cocaine destined for Guatemala, was arrested in northern Guayaquil. The same transnational operation netted Wilson Eduardo Mendoza Barberán — a former senior Guatemalan water-secretariat official linked to Los Choneros — in Guatemala, and Elixandro Ico Cuervo, an alleged liaison to FARC dissidents, in Colombia.

SourceInfobae

Colombia — 4.5 tonnes of cocaine seized in Putumayo and Norte de Santander

4.5 t cocaine + coca basePutumayo & Norte de Santander, ColombiaNone reported

Authorities seized 4.5 tonnes of cocaine plus 300 kilos and 220 gallons of coca base in operations against the ELN, the Coordinadora Nacional Ejército Bolivariano and the Comandos de Frontera. The load was headed for Central America; officials put the financial hit to the groups at roughly $85 million and about 7.7 million doses.

SourceChicanoticias
