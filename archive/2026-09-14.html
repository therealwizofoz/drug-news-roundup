Florida's Washington County calls a 16-month investigation its largest meth case ever, charges 15

1.4 kg methamphetamineWashington County, Florida15 charged

Sheriff Kevin Crews said "Operation King of the Hill" — a joint investigation by his office, the Florida Department of Law Enforcement, the DEA and the Georgia Bureau of Investigation that ran from March 2025 to June 2026 — has produced felony charges against 15 people accused of moving methamphetamine from North Georgia into the rural Florida Panhandle county, which he called the largest meth investigation in the county's history. Investigators traced local distribution to Wendy Holloway and Issac Noss, who were stopped by Florida Highway Patrol on June 8, 2026 with about 3 pounds of methamphetamine after a supply run to Georgia, and from there to alleged North Georgia suppliers Tekoa Ross and Edgar Soto; the sheriff's office estimated the ring moved 50 to 70 pounds of methamphetamine into the county over the life of the case.

Three Ohio sheriffs close a three-week sweep with six arrests and five guns off the street

Franklin County, Ohio6 arrested

The Franklin, Union and Licking County sheriff's offices announced September 13 that a joint three-week operation targeting narcotics dealers based in Franklin County, who investigators said were supplying surrounding counties, ended with six arrests and five firearms seized. Searches also recovered roughly 10 grams of fentanyl and 3 grams of heroin, which the Franklin County Sheriff's Office framed as part of an ongoing effort to disrupt drug distribution networks across central Ohio.

3South America

Colombian Navy runs down a cocaine go-fast boat in the central Caribbean, seizes 865 kilos rigged with a Starlink terminal

865 kg cocaineCaribbean Sea, Colombia1 arrested

Colombia's Navy intercepted a speeding "go-fast" boat in the central Caribbean on September 13 after its crew spotted the patrol and tried to outrun it toward the coast. Boarding teams found 29 sacks holding 861 packages of cocaine hydrochloride, along with a Starlink satellite antenna, a satellite phone and GPS units the smugglers had been using to navigate undetected; one man was captured aboard and taken to Cartagena, and the Navy valued the seized cocaine at more than $40 million.

Source724 Noticias

Colombian traffic police pry open a false floor and find 420 kilos of cocaine bound for the Antioquia border

420 kg cocaineRisaralda, Colombia

National Police traffic officers stopped a pickup truck on September 13 at kilometer 21 of the Barro Blanco sector in Quinchia, Risaralda, on the highway linking Caldas, Risaralda and Antioquia, and found 420 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride hidden inside a false floor built into the truck bed. The driver abandoned the vehicle and escaped into nearby woods before officers could make an arrest; authorities valued the drugs at more than 1.6 billion pesos and referred the case to narcotics prosecutors in Bogota.

Source724 Noticias

Chilean prosecutors use a controlled delivery to unravel a 1.6-tonne cannabis run from Antofagasta toward Santiago

1,600 kg cannabisAntofagasta, Chile4 arrested

Chile's OS7 narcotics unit and the Supra-Regional Prosecutor's Office spent months building a case before making arrests around September 10, when they moved on a shipment of more than 1,600 kilograms of cannabis that had entered the country through the Antofagasta region for delivery to the Santiago metropolitan area. Rather than a roadside stop, investigators relied on surveillance and a controlled-delivery technique to map the network before detaining four people described by officials as two Venezuelan and two Colombian nationals, who allegedly served as transporters, armed security and a financier.

SourceLa Hora

4Rest of World

Nigeria's drug agency intercepts UK-mailed synthetic-cannabis parcels in an eight-state, 2.3-tonne sweep

2.3 tonnes assorted drugsmultiple states, Nigeriaseveral arrested

Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency reported operations across Lagos, Ondo, Edo, Delta, Kaduna, Plateau, Bayelsa and Kano states between September 9 and 13 that recovered more than 2.3 tonnes of assorted drugs, including cannabis, the synthetic cannabinoid known locally as "Colos," and quantities of pregabalin, tramadol, diazepam and MDMA. Among the hauls were two parcels of synthetic cannabis mailed from the United Kingdom to Lagos's Murtala Muhammed International Airport, weighing about 32 kilograms and 12 kilograms; agency operatives arrested several suspects across the affected states, including a real estate agent and a tour agent accused of receiving the UK shipments.

SourcePRNigeria

Philippine agents raid a Taytay safehouse, seize a small arsenal and shabu worth ₱40.8 million

Taytay, Rizal, Philippines3 arrested

PDEA and police agents raided a house in Sitio Dulong Bangiad, Barangay San Juan, in Taytay, Rizal, at 5:30 a.m. on September 14 during a buy-bust operation, arresting three men known by the aliases "Diogen," "Muhammad" and "Eddienel" and seizing shabu authorities valued at about 40.8 million pesos. Officers also recovered an M1 carbine, an M16 rifle, four .45-caliber pistols and ammunition; a fourth suspect, alias "Abdila," fled before agents arrived and is now the subject of a manhunt.

SourceManila Bulletin

Costa Rican police raid a house near the Panama border and find 312 kilos of cocaine

312 kg cocaineCanoas, Costa Rica

Acting on an anonymous tip about a suspicious vehicle, Costa Rica's Drug Control Police and the Public Force searched a home in the Mariposa area of Canoas, near the border with Panama, around September 10 and found 312 kilograms of cocaine. Drug Control Police director Stephen Madden confirmed the seizure; no arrests were reported in connection with the find.

SourceInfobae Costa Rica

Assam Rifles breaks up two Myanmar-border smuggling runs in Mizoram, seizing meth tablets and heroin

6.99 kg methamphetamineMizoram, India2 arrested

Assam Rifles troops, working with Mizoram's Excise and Narcotics Department and the Narcotics Control Bureau, intercepted two vehicles in the Seling area of Aizawl district overnight on September 11-12 and recovered 6.991 kilograms of methamphetamine tablets, arresting two suspected couriers. In a separate operation the same night at Lungvar in Khawzawl district, troops acting on intelligence about a narcotics transshipment recovered 382 grams of heroin; officials valued the two hauls together at more than 6 crore rupees.
