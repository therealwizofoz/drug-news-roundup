Colombian army seizes 1,198 kilos of cocaine from a truck in Meta and ties two arrests to a FARC dissident unit

1,198 kg cocainePuerto Gaitán, Meta, Colombia2 arrested

Soldiers from the Fourth Division and Seventh Brigade stopped a tractor-trailer in Puerto Gaitán, Meta, on October 8 and found 1,198 kilos of cocaine. Two people were caught in the act; the government links them to the Amazonas bloc's 39th structure, a dissident faction attributed to alias Iván Mordisco.

4Rest of World

Guardia Civil finds nearly 3 tonnes of suspected cocaine and four Kalashnikovs at a rural house in Guadalajara, Spain

~2,900 kg cocaineGuadalajara, Spain8 detained

A joint Guadalajara and Madrid operation turned up about 2,775 kg of a substance that field-tested positive for cocaine at a rural property, plus smaller loads in a hidden-compartment car and a truck trailer. Eight people were detained and seven jailed; officers also took four assault rifles and €28,000 in cash. Raid dates were not given.

SourceEl Español

Mexican Navy and US Coast Guard intercept a boat 366 nautical miles off Oaxaca carrying about 1.2 tonnes of cocaine

~1,268 kg cocainePacific off Oaxaca, Mexico6 detained

A patrol and surveillance flight spotted a blue vessel in the Pacific south of Huatulco, and the Navy seized 46 bundles weighing roughly 1,268 kg. Six people were detained, and the load was taken to Puerto Chiapas. Security chief Omar García Harfuch announced the result.

SourceADN Sureste

Costa Rican border police intercept a boat with 103 bundles of apparent cocaine in the Térraba-Sierpe wetland

103 bundles cocaineTérraba-Sierpe, Costa RicaNo arrests

Officers chased the vessel on October 11 between Punta Mala and the Coronado estuary; the crew abandoned it and fled. Authorities say the count is still running and the haul could pass three tonnes, and they are hunting the occupants.

SourceLa Nación (Costa Rica)

Dominican drug agents seize 228 packages of suspected cocaine at a Santo Domingo port and Punta Cana airport

228 packages cocaineDominican Republic2 arrested

At Don Diego port, 203 packages were found in a false floor of a trailer bound for Puerto Rico after a ten-hour search, and one man was detained. At Punta Cana, 25 packages in a suitcase headed for Paris led to the arrest of a 34-year-old Dutch citizen. DEA and other agencies supported; weights await forensic analysis.
