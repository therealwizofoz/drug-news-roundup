1National / Federal (US)

Washington state fentanyl courier gets nine years for supplying a family-run trafficking ring

Federal Way / Seattle, Washington1 sentenced

A federal judge in Seattle sentenced Sir Terrique Milam, 22, to nine years in prison on September 3 for distributing fentanyl on behalf of the Jackson family drug trafficking organization based in Federal Way. Milam was recorded arranging a 150,000-pill fentanyl pickup in Arizona, and a 2024 search of his home found 323.8 grams of fentanyl under his bed; the wider investigation into the organization recovered more than 846,000 fentanyl pills, about seven kilograms combined of fentanyl powder and cocaine, and 29 firearms.

SourceFederal Way Mirror

2Local US Arrests

Minnesota — Golden Valley apartment search turns up 27 kilos of meth and $17,000 cash

27 kg methamphetamineGolden Valley, Minnesota1 arrested

Hennepin County's West Metro Drug Task Force searched an apartment on the 6500 block of Golden Valley Road on August 25 and found about 27 kilograms of methamphetamine and roughly 883 grams of cocaine, along with digital scales, cell phones and $16,976 in cash. Eric Prince Holton Jr., 44, was arrested and held on $500,000 bail after admitting the drugs and money were his.

SourceHoodline

Tennessee — an 8-year-old is pulled from a Munford steroid lab, three arrested

Munford, Tennessee3 arrested

A search warrant executed August 28 at a home in Munford, Tennessee found an active anabolic-steroid manufacturing operation with an 8-year-old child present. Deputies recovered about 11 kilograms of steroid compounds, a pound of psilocybin mushrooms, more than 500 counterfeit Xanax pills and over 500 pre-loaded vials, plus marijuana and firearms. David Adam McNeil, 39, faces the most serious charges, including aggravated child abuse, and is held on $500,000 bond; Amanda Carol McNeil and David Lee McNeil were also charged and released on bond.

SourceAction News 5

Massachusetts — Worcester man caught with a four-drug haul after kicking a police cruiser

Worcester, Massachusetts1 arrested

Worcester's Neighborhood Response Team stopped Salvatore Venuti, 46, on Madison Street on September 4 after seeing him holding what appeared to be a chunk of crack cocaine; he tried to flee and kicked an officer's cruiser during the arrest. Officers recovered about 12 grams of fentanyl, 35 grams of crack cocaine, 43 grams of oxycodone pills and a bag of crystal methamphetamine, along with a digital scale, cash and a replica firearm.

SourceHoodline

3South America

Ecuador — 2 tonnes of cocaine found in a fruit container bound for Sweden

2,040 kg cocaineMachala, Ecuador0 reported

Ecuadorian National Police inspecting a shipping container at Machala's maritime port on September 6 found 2,040 blocks of cocaine weighing about 2,040 kilograms, hidden among a fruit exporter's cargo destined for Sweden. Interior Minister John Reimberg announced the seizure; no arrests were reported and the exporting company was not named.

SourcePrimicias

Brazil — twin cocaine busts net 521 kilos in São Paulo and Santa Catarina in one day

521 kg cocaineBarra do Turvo, São Paulo / Itajaí, Santa Catarina, Brazil2 arrested

Brazil's Federal Police stopped a vehicle in Barra do Turvo, São Paulo, on September 6 and found about 290 kilograms of cocaine, and separately searched a home in Itajaí, Santa Catarina, the same day, discovering roughly 231 kilograms hidden in an underground storage bunker. Two people were arrested, including the Itajaí property's caretaker; investigators linked the finds to a case connected to a 3.5-tonne cocaine seizure from July.

SourceND Mais

Venezuela — 800 kilos of marijuana seized in more than 1,500 packages on Cubagua island

800 kg cannabisCubagua, Nueva Esparta, Venezuela

Venezuela's Interior Ministry said National Bolivarian Police seized 1,529 packages of marijuana weighing about 800 kilograms on Cubagua island, Nueva Esparta state, the result of continued follow-up on an earlier investigation. The seizure was announced September 6.

SourceÚltimas Noticias

4Rest of World

Mexico — Navy dismantles three more meth labs in Guerrero, days after the Sinaloa haul

Guerrero, Mexico

Mexico's Navy disabled three clandestine methamphetamine labs on September 6 — two in Zirándaro de los Chávez and one in Coahuayutla de José María Izazaga, Guerrero — seizing roughly 2,400 kilograms of chemical product and about 1,000 kilograms of precursor chemicals, along with more than 260 industrial drums, reactors and distillers. The operation, run with the federal prosecutor's office and aided by U.S. intelligence sharing, followed the Navy's dismantling of three larger labs in Sinaloa earlier in the week.

SourceLa Razón

Nigeria — cannabis hidden in a hollowed-out fuel tanker seized in Ondo State

2.1 t cannabisOndo State, Nigeria

Nigeria's National Drug Law Enforcement Agency intercepted a 20,000-litre fuel tanker on the Akoko/Lokoja Expressway on September 4 after its driver refused to stop, then found a false compartment holding 165 bags of cannabis weighing about 2,145 kilograms alongside the tanker's legitimate fuel cargo. The driver abandoned the truck at a church in Ugbe Akoko and fled on foot; the seizure was part of a wider weeklong crackdown across nine Nigerian states.

SourceChannels Television

Thailand — courier caught asleep at a car wash with 42,000 methamphetamine pills

Kalasin, Thailand1 arrested

Police in Kalasin municipality were called to a car wash after a bystander noticed a man asleep inside his car in the early hours of September 7; officers found Ekaphon Changprasert, 39, unresponsive at the wheel with 12,000 methamphetamine pills in the vehicle and a further 30,000 pills in his rented hotel room, plus a modified handgun. He told investigators he had gone seven days without sleep ferrying the drugs for a Laotian trafficking network in exchange for a 70,000-baht fee.

SourceKhaosod English
