1National / Federal (US)

US Attorney's 'Summer Crackdown' nets 60 federal indictments and 160 kilos of drugs in DC

160 kg drugs, 533 firearmsWashington, D.C.60+ indicted

U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro announced results on Sept. 21 of a summer-long, multi-agency enforcement push in Washington, D.C., which produced federal indictments against more than 60 defendants — roughly half on narcotics charges and half on felon-in-possession gun charges — plus 28 gang-member arrests. Working with the Metropolitan Police Department, FBI, DEA, Homeland Security Investigations and ATF, the operation seized 533 illegal firearms along with about 64.1 kilograms of methamphetamine, 50.1 kilograms of cocaine (including crack) and 3 kilograms of fentanyl.

SourceU.S. Attorney's Office for D.C.

2Local US Arrests

Lima, Ohio SWAT raid turns up 2.5 kilograms of methamphetamine and $18,000 in cash

2.5 kg methamphetamineLima, Ohio1 arrested

The Allen County Sheriff's Office SWAT team and the West Central Ohio Crime Task Force served a search warrant Sept. 21 at a Harding Highway home in Lima, finding about 2.5 kilograms of methamphetamine and more than $18,000 in cash. Dameon Pearson, 49, was arrested and booked into the Allen County Jail on a felony drug-possession charge.

SourceAllen County Sheriff's Office / Hometown Stations

Worcester, Massachusetts vice raid breaks up a Main South crack and fentanyl operation

78 g crack, 33 g fentanylWorcester, Massachusetts4 charged

Worcester police vice detectives and the gang unit searched a Preston Street apartment on Sept. 21 after a lengthy investigation into crack and fentanyl sales in the Main South neighborhood, seizing about 78 grams of packaged crack cocaine and 33 grams of fentanyl along with scales and cash. Four people, including three 18-year-olds and a 17-year-old, were charged with trafficking.

SourceWorcester Police Department / Newport Dispatch

3South America

Ecuador finds a tonne of cocaine hidden in export machinery bound for Guatemala

~1 t cocainePosorja, Ecuador1 arrested

Ecuadorian police inspecting a shipping container at the port of Posorja, near Guayaquil, on Sept. 22 found 995 blocks of cocaine — roughly a metric tonne — concealed inside machinery declared for export to Guatemala. Héctor Mendoza, president of the exporting company, was arrested and turned over to judicial authorities.

SourcePolicía Nacional del Ecuador / Infobae

Brazilian highway police pull 975 kilos of cocaine, paste and marijuana from a Mato Grosso do Sul truck

975 kg mixed drugsMiranda, Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil1 detained

Federal Highway Police stopped a truck hauling iron ore at kilometer 600 of BR-262 in Miranda, Mato Grosso do Sul, before dawn on Sept. 22 and found narcotics hidden in a second trailer atop the ore load: 543 kilograms of compressed marijuana, 218.2 kilograms of coca paste and 213.4 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride. The driver said he had been paid to haul the load to Campo Grande and was turned over to federal police.

SourcePolícia Rodoviária Federal / A Gazeta de Dourados

Brazilian federal police freeze $50 million tied to a Paraguay-linked cocaine pipeline

R$50M in assets frozenMato Grosso do Sul, São Paulo and Paraná, Brazil

Federal Police launched 'Operação Suécia XXXV' on Sept. 22, serving 15 search-and-seizure warrants across Mato Grosso do Sul, São Paulo and Paraná and freezing up to 50 million reais in real estate, vehicles, jewelry and bank accounts belonging to a criminal organization accused of moving cocaine into Brazil from Paraguay. Investigators tied the group to at least 1,302 kilograms of cocaine already recovered in four earlier, separately reported seizures.

SourcePolícia Federal

Argentine gendarmerie dog sniffs out 53 kilos of marijuana in a Santiago del Estero vehicle

53 kg cannabisSantiago del Estero, Argentina

A Gendarmería Nacional detection dog named Yasi flagged a vehicle on Route 16 in Santiago del Estero province on Sept. 22, leading officers to marijuana hidden in secret compartments. A federal court ordered the seizure of more than 53 kilograms of the drug along with the vehicle.

SourceGendarmería Nacional Argentina / Canal26

4Rest of World

Hong Kong national arrested with 350 kilos of crystal meth staged for export from Bangkok

350 kg methamphetamineBangkok, Thailand1 arrested

Thai Crime Suppression Division officers raided a house in Bangkok's Min Buri district on Sept. 22 after a tip about a Hong Kong-run storage operation, finding about 350 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine along with packaging equipment. A 26-year-old Hong Kong man, who told police he had arrived on a tourist visa from Kuala Lumpur just over a week earlier, was arrested; officers valued the haul at roughly 700 million baht.

SourceRoyal Thai Police / The Pattaya News, Bangkok Post

'Project Ghostwire' dismantles a Canadian dark-web drug marketplace, seizing nine kinds of narcotics

30+ kg mixed drugsMississauga / York Region, Ontario, Canada5 arrested

York Regional Police announced Sept. 22 the results of a months-long probe, launched in January, into a dark-web and encrypted-app marketplace called 'Icy White North' that was run out of a Mississauga penthouse and served customers across Canada. Five suspects from the Greater Toronto Area face 81 charges after investigators seized about $3 million in drugs and luxury goods, including 5.5 kilograms of fentanyl, 5.5 kilograms of methamphetamine, 5.4 kilograms of MDMA, 4.5 kilograms of ketamine, roughly 3.7 kilograms of cocaine and crack combined, 700 grams of heroin, and thousands of pills of LSD, Xanax, oxycodone and hydromorphone.

SourceYork Regional Police / CBC News

Thai highway police seize 7.6 million meth pills hidden inside a generator

7.6M meth pillsNakhon Sawan, Thailand4 arrested

Highway police in Nakhon Sawan province broke up a smuggling network overnight into Sept. 22, finding 7.6 million methamphetamine pills concealed inside a generator. Four suspected gang members were arrested at separate locations, along with two pickup trucks and an SUV used to move the shipment.

SourceRoyal Thai Police / Bangkok Post
