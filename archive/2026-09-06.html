SOUTHCOM boards and sinks a fourth Los Choneros refueling vessel in nine days — no strike, no deaths

Eastern Pacific

On September 5, Marines and sailors launched from the USS San Antonio boarded a "multi-vessel" floating refueling station — a mother boat towing smaller fuel craft — used to resupply drug runs for Ecuador's Los Choneros gang in the Eastern Pacific, removed the crew, transferred them to Ecuadorian authorities and sank the vessels once cleared. It was the fourth such non-lethal boarding of a Los Choneros refueling vessel since August 28, after nearly identical operations on September 2 and 3, a run of boat-and-search actions distinct from the lethal missile strikes that have killed 227 people over the past year and drawn sustained congressional criticism.

Florida — Escambia County traffic stop for a tag violation turns up 4.7 pounds of fentanyl

2.1 kg fentanylEscambia County, Florida2 arrested

A deputy stopped a car in the Wedgewood area of Escambia County on September 2 for an expired tag, and a K-9 alert led to a search that turned up about 2.1 kilograms of fentanyl and $22,874 in cash. Driver Ramon Becerra Urbie, 24, who is in the country illegally, and passenger Adrian Medina-Ortiz, 25, were both charged with fentanyl trafficking and are being held without bond.

Massachusetts — Southbridge apartment raid nets a five-drug haul and five arrests

53 g fentanyl, 63 g cocaineSouthbridge, Massachusetts5 arrested

Southbridge police, backed by a Webster K-9 unit, searched a Dean Street apartment on September 2 and found about 53 grams of fentanyl, 63 grams of crack cocaine, additional powder cocaine, methamphetamine, prescription pills, packaging materials and $3,864 in suspected drug proceeds. Five people were arrested, including Emmanuel Soto and Vianca Rodriguez, who each face trafficking charges for heroin/fentanyl and cocaine along with unlawful ammunition possession.

Ohio — Cincinnati detectives' Westwood raid nets $230,000 and a Major Drug Offender charge

Cincinnati, Ohio1 arrested

An investigation into trafficking along Cincinnati's Court Street corridor led detectives to a Westwood home on September 1, where they found more than $230,000 in cash, two firearms and enough narcotics to qualify the case under Ohio's Major Drug Offender statute, which carries a mandatory 3-to-11-year sentence. Andre Neal, 47, was arrested on felony drug trafficking charges.

3South America

Colombia — army and police dismantle a cocaine lab outside Cúcuta, seize 790 kilos of cocaine and its base

490 kg cocaine, 300 kg coca baseCúcuta, Colombia0 reported

Colombia's National Army and National Police raided a cocaine-processing site in rural Cúcuta, Norte de Santander, on September 5, seizing about 490 kilograms of finished cocaine hydrochloride and 300 kilograms of cocaine base along with more than 1,000 gallons of liquid precursor chemicals and 200 kilograms of solid precursors. Authorities put the hit to the criminal network's finances at roughly 2.5 billion pesos and said it would have kept about 1.2 million doses off the market; no arrests were reported.

SourceChicanoticias

Brazil — a family's cocaine run ends when their truck bogs down in a São Paulo cane field

548 kg cocaineTupi Paulista, São Paulo, Brazil4 arrested

Federal Highway Police spotted a truck and pickup pulled over on the SP-563 in Tupi Paulista on September 3; when the driver saw them he drove the loaded truck into a sugarcane field, jumped out and ran, leaving his wife, son and daughter behind in the pickup. Officers found about 547.95 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride aboard the abandoned truck, and the driver was caught and arrested the next morning along with his three relatives.

SourcePerfil News

Uruguay — a small plane drops 400 kilos of cocaine and paste over Artigas, seven caught waiting for it

305 kg cocaine, 103 kg coca baseArtigas, Uruguay7 arrested

Investigators who had tracked the smuggling ring since February were waiting near Baltasar Brum, Artigas, when a small aircraft with Bolivian registration dropped roughly 305 kilograms of cocaine and 103 kilograms of coca paste as part of "Operación Polux"; seven people were arrested at the scene, five of whom were later charged and jailed until March 2027. Police said the drugs, believed to have originated in Peru, were valued at about $3 million on the Uruguayan market.

SourceSubrayado

Argentina — Gendarmerie finds cocaine and marijuana stuffed inside capybara plush toys on a shopping-tour bus

50 kg cannabis, 4 kg cocaineCatamarca, Argentina1 detained

National Gendarmerie officers stopped a bus running a cross-border "shopping tour" from Orán to Mendoza on Route 38 in Catamarca on September 3 and noticed that several capybara plush toys a passenger was carrying were unusually heavy and smelled of marijuana. Testing confirmed about 49.9 kilograms of marijuana and 4.3 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride hidden inside the toys, and the woman was detained.

4Rest of World

Mexico — Navy destroys three Sinaloa meth labs and nearly 17 tonnes of methamphetamine

16.8 t methamphetamineSinaloa, Mexico0 reported

Mexico's Navy, working with federal prosecutors, raided three clandestine labs around Corralejo and Corral Viejo, Sinaloa, on September 2 and 3, disabling a 15,000-square-meter processing site and seizing methamphetamine in various stages of production along with more than 4,300 liters of liquid precursor chemicals and 5,700 kilograms of solid production materials. Officials put the combined haul at about 16.8 tonnes of methamphetamine and estimated the damage to trafficking finances at over $347 million; no arrests were announced.

SourceInfobae

Europe — Eurojust dismantles a Romanian "crime as a service" trucking ring that moved drugs for other gangs

114 kg cocaine, 1.3 t hashishRomania7 arrested

A Eurojust-coordinated raid across 16 properties in Romania on August 24 broke up a criminal group that had rented out truck-based smuggling since 2023, hiding shipments in false compartments for other trafficking organizations and laundering the proceeds through front companies. French, German, Italian and Spanish police helped trace the network through encrypted SkyECC messages, and investigators recovered about 114 kilograms of cocaine, 228 kilograms of cannabis and 1,300 kilograms of hashish worth an estimated €25 million; seven suspects were arrested, including one Europol high-value target.

SourceEurojust

India — NCB intercepts two methamphetamine shipments on the Myanmar border in one night

22 kg methamphetamineMizoram / Tripura, India4 arrested

The Narcotics Control Bureau's Aizawl and Agartala units, working with local police, stopped a Myanmar national near the Zokhawthar-Champhai border crossing overnight on September 2-3 with about 14.26 kilograms of methamphetamine tablets, and separately seized 8.04 kilograms of methamphetamine and Yaba tablets hidden under a vehicle's back seat in Tripura. Four people were arrested across the two operations, which authorities valued at a combined ₹17.84 crore.

SourceNortheast Today
