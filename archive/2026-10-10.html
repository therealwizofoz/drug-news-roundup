Ecuador finds 1.6 tonnes of cocaine inside an industrial metal separator at Guayaquil, bound for Mexico

1.68 t cocaineGuayaquil, Ecuador2 detained

Police at the Port of Guayaquil opened an export container and found 1,684 blocks of cocaine, about 1,676 kg net, concealed in a machine declared as a metal separator. Interior Minister John Reimberg announced the find on October 9; the exporter's manager and legal representative were detained.

SourceVistazo

Colombian police pull 295 kilos of cocaine from a cocoa-cake container at Cartagena, headed from Peru to Spain

295 kg cocaineCartagena, Colombia

Officers at the port of Cartagena found the cocaine hidden in a shipment of cocoa cake that originated in Peru and was destined for Spain. The report mentions no arrests.

SourceEl Universal

Colombia's government reports 611 kilos of cocaine in Ibagué and a tonne of marijuana in Mitú in a single day

611.8 kg cocaine, 1,058 kg marijuanaTolima and Vaupés, Colombia6 arrested

In a 24-hour tally announced by President Abelardo de la Espriella, police stopped a truck carrying 611.8 kg of cocaine in Ibagué, Tolima, and arrested its driver. In Mitú, Vaupés, five suspected members of the 'Armando Ríos' structure were captured with 1,058 kg of marijuana and two boats. The figures are government-reported.

SourceEl Universal

4Rest of World

Spain and Ecuador arrest 21, including two serving officers, in Operation Machala cocaine network takedown

~2.1 t cocaineSpain and Ecuador21 arrested

About 130 officers carried out 23 simultaneous searches in both countries, seizing 630 kg of cocaine and a second stash of roughly 1.5 tonnes in this phase. Prosecutors allege a detained Spanish police inspector hid 20 million euros in cash at his Madrid home; the wider case covers about 42.5 tonnes of cocaine, with branches in Belgium, Portugal, the Netherlands and the UAE.

SourceMoncloa
