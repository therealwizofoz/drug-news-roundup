Edition O'028

Drug News Roundup

Monday, September 21, 2026 · 10 stories

Covering reporting published since the Sept 20 edition, roughly Sept 17-21, with several local U.S. busts running back to incident dates of Sept 17-18 where reporting lagged. No second SOUTHCOM strike was found beyond the Sept 19 Caribbean strike already covered in yesterday's edition, so the campaign's running total of 69 strikes and at least 231 deaths carries forward unchanged. Several candidate stories were checked and dropped as stale or unverifiable: a widely syndicated "DEA agents seize 300+ pounds of cocaine" story datelined Sept 20 in west-suburban Chicago is the same Cook County case (339 lbs, $700,000 cash, two men charged) already logged on Sept 19; a "DEA seizes 3,552 lbs of methamphetamine in Fontana/Norco" story recirculating this week was actually a 2022 case; a Sacramento meth traffic-stop story dated on its face to Sept 18 was in fact from Sept 18, 2025; a Dayton, Ohio 11-kg fentanyl/cocaine safe seizure was a March 2026 case; a DEA-Seattle social media post claiming a 3,284-kg cocaine seizure off Ecuador gave no date or location and could not be verified as current; and reporting on a Defense Secretary order to strike survivors of a boat strike, and congressional calls for his resignation, traces back to a Sept. 2, 2025 incident and a Washington Post report from late 2025 that continues to generate follow-up coverage, not a new development from this window. Colombia's Sept. 19 nationwide 24-hour tally (2,411 kg marijuana, 900 kg cocaine, 75 kg coca base) and the government's cumulative 40.6-tonne, 44-day Armada figure were both left out of today's tally as vaguer aggregates layered on top of seizures already counted in prior editions; only the specific, dated Colombia and Brazil events below are counted. Ecuador's Manta curfew seizure is carried in the ongoing section with only the new Sept 20 tonnage counted toward the tally, since the initial 1.51-tonne find was already logged and tallied on Sept 20.

San Ysidro border officers pull 181 pounds of cocaine from a hidden vehicle compartment

181 lbs cocaine (82 kg)San Diego, California

CBP officers at the San Ysidro Port of Entry in San Diego found roughly 181 pounds of cocaine hidden in a non-factory compartment of a vehicle during a secondary inspection on Sept. 18. San Diego Field Operations Director Sidney Aki announced the seizure on social media; CBP did not release the driver's identity or say whether an arrest followed.

SourceCBP / Hoodline

2Local US Arrests

Massachusetts troopers pull 20 kilograms of fentanyl and $42,000 from a Lawrence house

20 kg fentanylLawrence, Massachusetts1 arrested

Massachusetts State Police's Commonwealth Interstate Narcotics Reduction Enforcement Team, working with Lawrence police, searched a residence on Sept. 17 and recovered about 20 kilograms of suspected fentanyl, roughly $42,000 in cash, and equipment for processing and packaging narcotics. Lawrence resident Miguel Soto-Castillo was arrested and charged with fentanyl possession following an investigation that began in August.

SourceMassachusetts State Police / Shrewsbury Post

Five-month 'No Pill Left Behind' probe nets three arrests and pressed fentanyl pills in Pinetops, NC

126 g fentanyl, 1,785 pillsPinetops, North Carolina3 arrested

North Carolina's State Bureau of Investigation, the FBI Safe Streets Task Force and ATF closed a five-month investigation on Sept. 17 with three arrests in Edgecombe County, seizing more than 126 grams of fentanyl, 1,785 pills pressed to mimic prescription medication, and three firearms. Shertavion Pitt, Caleb Davis and Vinsa Phillips face trafficking and distribution charges; Phillips is additionally charged with distributing within 1,000 feet of a school.

SourceNC SBI / WCTI

3South America

Colombian army captures La Cordillera's hitman chief 'alias Cuba' in Risaralda

Dosquebradas, Risaralda, Colombia1 captured

The Colombian Army's Gaula Militar, the Prosecutor's office and national police captured a man known as "alias Cuba," identified as head of hitmen for the La Cordillera criminal band, in Dosquebradas, Risaralda, on Sept. 20. Authorities linked him to homicides, extortion and micro-trafficking against merchants and transporters in the Pereira metropolitan area, and said a related sweep against the group's armed wing has captured 15 more members and more than 20 firearms.

SourceColombian Army / Infobae Colombia

Brazilian police find 708 kilos of pressed marijuana hidden in a Toledo farmhouse attic

708 kg cannabisToledo, Paraná, Brazil1 detained

An integrated team from the Federal Police, Paraná's FICCO task force and the Military Police's BPFRON searched a rural property in Toledo, Paraná, on Sept. 20 after an anonymous tip and found about 708 kilograms of pressed marijuana in blue-wrapped bundles hidden in an attic space, along with two firearms. One resident was detained during the search.

SourcePolícia Federal / CATVE

4Rest of World

Gunfire erupts as a Vancouver Island task force seizes 19 kilograms of fentanyl in Victoria

19 kg fentanylVictoria, British Columbia, Canada

British Columbia's Combined Forces Special Enforcement Unit and the RCMP carried out a drug-trafficking search in Victoria on Sept. 18 that recovered about 19 kilograms of suspected fentanyl, two loaded firearms and a cattle prod; a gun was discharged during the operation, though no injuries were reported. Cash and cellphones tied to the alleged trafficking were also seized.

SourceCFSEU-BC / CTV News

Thai police unravel a Laos-linked meth network after two Buddhist monks are caught with pills

49,400 meth pillsBuriram, Thailand7 arrested

A Sept. 18 traffic stop in Buriram province turned up two monks carrying methamphetamine pills, and the ensuing investigation by provincial police, border patrol and soldiers rolled up a wider network, ultimately seizing about 49,400 meth pills and arresting seven people, including four monks who were defrocked. Investigators said phone messages pointed to a Laos-based supplier directing distribution across several Buriram and Khon Kaen districts, and the temple's abbot admitted using temple funds to buy pills.

SourceKhaosod English

Belgian customs find cocaine dissolved into liquid soap bound from Colombia to Croatia

Antwerp, Belgium

Antwerp customs intercepted a container of 450 boxes of liquid soap shipped from Colombia on Aug. 24 after manipulated container ID numbers drew suspicion; testing confirmed the soap held dissolved cocaine, though prosecutors said lab analysis to determine the exact quantity was still underway when the find was disclosed on Sept. 20. The shipment had been due to continue by road to Croatia.

SourceAntwerp Public Prosecutor's Office / Brussels Morning

OR Tambo Airport nets its third suspected drug mule in three weeks, this one from Ghana

OR Tambo Airport, South Africa1 arrested

A 52-year-old South African man arriving from Accra was stopped in an intelligence-led search at OR Tambo International Airport on Sept. 20 and found carrying bullet-shaped packages of suspected heroin valued at more than 2 million rand. He is the third suspected courier arrested at the airport in as many weeks and is due in Kempton Park Magistrates' Court on trafficking charges.

SourceSouth African Police Service / EWN

Ecuador's Manta curfew sweep finds a second buried cocaine stash, pushing the haul to 2.9 tonnes

1.41 t cocaine (new)Manta, Ecuador

Building on the 1.51-tonne find reported in yesterday's edition, Ecuadorian police on Sept. 20 broke into a second hidden storage site in the same Pacoche-Ligüiqui sector of Manta and recovered 42 more jute sacks holding 1,250 cocaine packages — about 1.41 additional tonnes — pushing the curfew campaign's cocaine total to 2.9 tonnes. Since the nighttime curfew began Sept. 17 across four provinces, police say they have arrested 289 people for violating it and 73 more on charges including extortion and organized crime.

SourcePolicía Nacional / Infobae
