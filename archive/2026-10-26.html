South African police seize about 250 kg of cocaine in five bags at Johannesburg's OR Tambo airport

250 kg cocaineJohannesburg, South Africa

Police announced on 25 October that 216 blocks of cocaine were found spread across five bags, with an estimated value above R200 million (about US$12 million). No arrests were reported in this case, and the origin and destination were not disclosed; police separately say 116 suspected drug mules have been arrested at the airport over two years.

SourceRio Times

Lithuanian police dismantle a large drug lab holding over 800 kg of meth and amphetamine after a Polish tip-off

500+ kg meth, 300 kg amphetamineLithuania

Police found more than 500 kg of methamphetamine and about 300 kg of amphetamine at the lab, reported on 25 October. An unusual chemical order placed in Łódź, Poland, led investigators to the network; no arrests were mentioned in the report.

SourceTVP World
