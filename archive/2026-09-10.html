SOUTHCOM kills three in 69th Caribbean strike as the campaign's death toll passes 230 amid mounting legal criticism

Caribbean Sea

On September 9, Joint Task Force Western Hemisphere carried out a lethal strike on a go-fast vessel that U.S. Southern Command said was moving drugs along an established Caribbean trafficking route, killing all three people aboard. It was the 69th publicly acknowledged strike in the year-old campaign — which independent tracking now puts at more than 230 people killed — and the third since the pace resumed in August following a roughly two-month pause that began in late June. Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International and independent United Nations experts have called the strikes unlawful extrajudicial killings, and four House Democrats — Reps. Gregory Meeks, Adam Smith, Jim Himes and Bennie Thompson — introduced a War Powers Resolution on September 2 seeking a vote to end them, an effort the Republican-controlled chambers have not brought to the floor.

SourceWashington Examiner / SOUTHCOM

2Local US Arrests

Massachusetts — twin Hyde Park searches net fentanyl, crack cocaine and a high-capacity magazine, two arrested

Hyde Park, Boston, Massachusetts2 arrested

Boston police searched two homes on Georgetowne Drive in the Hyde Park neighborhood on September 8 and arrested 26-year-old Joshua Lewis and 41-year-old Brian Lender. Investigators reported recovering crack cocaine, marijuana, bagged fentanyl, an unidentified bag of white powder, rifle and shotgun ammunition, an illegal high-capacity magazine and more than $1,200 in cash between the two addresses; Lewis was charged with trafficking and intent to distribute, while Lender faces weapons and multiple drug-possession counts.

SourceDorchester Post

3South America

Bolivia — highway stop nets 26.6 kilos of cocaine as a wider crackdown grounds more than 80 trafficking aircraft

26.6 kg cocaineChallapata-Uyuni highway, Bolivia

Bolivia's anti-narcotics police (FELCN) stopped a vehicle on the Challapata-Uyuni highway on September 8 and found 35 packages of cocaine weighing about 26.6 kilograms. Officials said the stop came amid a broader campaign that has impounded more than 80 small aircraft tied to drug flights after forensic testing confirmed their use, dismantled several cocaine-hydrochloride labs, and led to the arrest of foreign nationals from Colombia, Brazil and Paraguay.

Sourceeju.tv

Argentina — two Peruvian women caught with cocaine hidden in hair-conditioner bottles and shoe insoles

9.66 kg cocaineSantiago del Estero, Argentina2 arrested

Gendarmería Nacional stopped two Peruvian women on Route 9 near the El Jume wind farm in Santiago del Estero province on September 7 and found 42 packages of cocaine — some concealed inside bottles of hair conditioner, others hidden beneath the insoles of their sneakers — weighing a combined 9.66 kilograms. Both women were arrested.

SourceArgentina.gob.ar

Brazil — Itajaí cocaine case grows to nearly 1.25 tonnes as Polícia Federal returns to the same investigation

725 kg cocaineItajaí, Santa Catarina, Brazil1 arrested

Two days after logging a 521-kilogram cocaine haul from Barra do Turvo and Itajaí, Polícia Federal went back to the same Santa Catarina investigation on September 8 and found another 725 kilograms hidden beneath the floor of a house in Itajaí's Cordeiros neighborhood, arresting one more suspect. The new find brings the case's running total to nearly 1.25 tonnes of cocaine tied to the same trafficking network.
