CJNG's Michoacán commanders — brothers "R1" and "R2" — indicted in Washington

Washington, D.C. / Michoacán, Mexico2 charged

A federal grand jury in the District of Columbia returned an indictment on August 26 charging Ramón Álvarez Ayala, 44, and Rafael Álvarez Ayala, 42, both Mexican nationals, with conspiring to manufacture and distribute five kilograms or more of cocaine and 500 grams or more of methamphetamine for importation into the United States between 2004 and December 2023, and with using, carrying, brandishing and discharging a firearm — including a machine gun — in furtherance of that conspiracy. The brothers, known as "R1" and "R2," were regional commanders for the Cártel de Jalisco Nueva Generación in Michoacán; Ramón was arrested by Mexican authorities in Atotonilco el Alto, Jalisco on July 30, 2026 and is held at the Altiplano federal prison. Each faces a mandatory minimum of 40 years and a maximum of two consecutive life sentences. The case is being investigated by the DEA's Special Operations Division Bilateral Investigations Unit in Los Angeles.

SourceU.S. Department of Justice

"Operation Last Call" — 44 arrested on federal gun and drug charges in Nashville

2+ kg meth · 1+ kg fentanyl · 150+ firearmsNashville, Tennessee44 arrested

Forty-four people were arrested on federal indictments over the weekend of August 19–23 in a joint initiative by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Middle District of Tennessee, ATF, the U.S. Marshals Service and Metro Nashville Police, announced August 24. Undercover agents made controlled gun and drug purchases in areas selected after a years-long review of shots-fired calls; the operation recovered more than 150 firearms, over 2 kg of methamphetamine, over 1 kg of fentanyl and more than half a kilogram of cocaine, along with multiple machine-gun conversion devices. Twenty-seven of the 44 had prior felony convictions and 10 were on probation, parole, supervised release or bond at the time.

SourceU.S. Department of Justice

Alabama — parking-lot hand-off in Semmes yields meth, cocaine, fentanyl and ten guns

5 lb meth · 1 kg cocaine · 21 g fentanyl · 10 gunsSemmes, Alabama2 charged

Mobile County sheriff's deputies working with Homeland Security Investigations broke up an alleged drug hand-off in a commercial parking lot in Semmes on August 25, seizing five pounds of suspected methamphetamine, one kilogram of suspected powder cocaine, 21 grams of suspected fentanyl and ten firearms — three semiautomatic rifles and seven handguns. Two high-speed pursuits followed. Dramico Monte Bailey Jr., 29, of Mobile is charged with drug trafficking, reckless endangerment and illegal possession of firearms; Kashunte Quintrell Tate, 28, of Prichard is charged with attempting to elude. Sheriff Paul Burch said the supply ran from South Florida and that further arrests are expected.

SourceFOX10 News

Ohio — two kilos of cocaine thrown from a fleeing car during a Richland County pursuit

~2 kg cocaineRichland County, Ohio2 arrested

The METRICH Enforcement Unit, assisted by the Richland County Sheriff's Office, ran a traffic enforcement operation on August 25 against two vehicles suspected of moving large quantities of cocaine near Mansfield. One driver stopped and was questioned; the second fled, and roughly two kilograms of cocaine were thrown from the car during the pursuit and later recovered along the route. Two men were arrested on felony drug charges, with child endangering charges added because a woman and a child were passengers in the fleeing vehicle.

3South America

Ecuador — 43 arrested and a smuggling plane seized as police hit Los Lobos and Los Choneros

~2.5 t drugs · aircraft · $1M + €858,000Guayas, Manabí, Esmeraldas and three other provinces, Ecuador43 arrested

Ecuador's National Police executed two simultaneous operations, codenamed "Factoría" and "Jericó," on the morning of August 26, carrying out 36 raids across the provinces of Guayas, Santo Domingo, El Oro, Los Ríos, Manabí and Esmeraldas. Interior Minister John Reimberg said 43 alleged members of Los Lobos and Los Choneros were detained, among them two Mexican nationals linked to the Jalisco New Generation Cartel who allegedly ran a clandestine air bridge — using unlicensed airstrips and light aircraft — to move cocaine from Ecuador toward North America. Seizures included roughly 2.5 tonnes of controlled substances, more than one million US dollars and €858,000 in cash, 25 firearms and about a thousand rounds of ammunition, 21 vehicles, an aircraft, close to a thousand litres of aviation fuel, 47 mobile phones and ballistic vests. The operations were coordinated with the Attorney General's Office with cooperation from the DEA and support from US Southern Command.

SourceInfobae / Expreso

Peru — 352 kg of cocaine seized after a chase on the Binational Highway in Moquegua

352.15 kg cocaineMoquegua, Peru4 detained

Peruvian National Police intercepted a vehicle on the Carretera Binacional in Moquegua on the night of August 25–26 and recovered 352 kilograms 150 grams of cocaine hydrochloride packed in brick-shaped bundles hidden beneath the seats and in compartments. A second vehicle running ahead as a lookout was also stopped. Four people were detained; police attribute the shipment to a criminal organisation known as "Los Maléficos del Sur."

SourceLa Prensa Regional

Brazil — 373 kg of skunk cannabis taken off a boat outside Rio Branco

373.5 kg cannabis (skunk)Rio Branco, Acre, Brazil

The Acre integrated organised-crime task force FICCO/AC seized 373.5 kilograms of skunk cannabis, along with a boat and its outboard motor, during operations in rural Rio Branco on August 25. Officers acted on anonymous tips about a vessel carrying illicit cargo in an area known as "Furo," near Ramal Caipora. The task force draws on the Federal Police and Acre's civil, military and penal police, with support from BOPE, the environmental policing battalion, the Xapuri civil police and the border special operations group GEFRON.

SourcePolícia Federal (FICCO/AC)

Argentina — Naval Prefecture pulls 340 kg of marijuana out of the Paraná at Puerto Iguazú

340+ kg cannabisPuerto Iguazú, Misiones, Argentina

Prefectura Naval Argentina reported on August 26 that it had seized more than 340 kilograms of marijuana, packed in 452 bundles inside 11 large sacks, at Puerto Carolina in Puerto Iguazú, at kilometre 1,865 of the Paraná River. Crews detected an incoming boat and recovered the cargo after a combined land and water search; the load was valued at about 1.3 billion pesos. The seizure falls under Plan Guaçurarí, the national border-control push in Misiones province. No arrests were reported.

SourceMinisterio de Seguridad (Argentina)

Anti-narcotics officers of the Policía Nacional Civil's SGAIA unit stopped a pickup truck at a checkpoint in Aldea San Isidro, in the municipality of Malacatán, San Marcos, on August 26 and found 202 packages of suspected cocaine inside a purpose-built compartment, detected with the help of a K-9 unit.
