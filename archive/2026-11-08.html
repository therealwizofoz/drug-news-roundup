Border Patrol checkpoint in San Clemente yields over 1,400 pounds of cocaine from two back-to-back vehicle stops

~651 kg cocaineSan Clemente, California2 arrested

Agents at the San Clemente checkpoint in California's San Diego Sector stopped two vehicles on 25 October and found about 550 vacuum-sealed packages of suspected cocaine, valued at roughly $9.8 million. Both drivers were arrested and the DEA took custody of them and the drugs; the case was publicized on 7 November.

SourceFox News

Coast Guard cutter William Trump brings ashore about 3,600 pounds of cocaine from two Caribbean interdictions

~1,643 kg cocaineCaribbean Sea / Port Everglades, Florida4 detained

The cutter's crew landed the haul on Wednesday 4 November, with the drugs valued near $27 million. Four detainees were handed to federal agents at Port Everglades, Florida.
