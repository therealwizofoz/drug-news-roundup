1National / Federal (US)

Rubio's Latin America tour ends with Peru joining a new US-backed anti-trafficking coalition and Ecuador gaining a Coast Guard cutter

Colombia, Ecuador, Peru

Secretary of State Marco Rubio closed a week-long tour of Colombia, Ecuador and Peru on September 10 by announcing, alongside newly elected President Keiko Fujimori, that Peru has joined the 'Shield of the Americas' — a Trump administration-backed coalition of more than a dozen countries organized against drug trafficking and organized crime. In Ecuador, Rubio's visit produced a pledge to transfer a US Coast Guard Island-class cutter and five interceptor boats to the Ecuadorian Navy, adding to earlier US drone donations meant to help Quito track trafficking routes through the Pacific, and both countries said they had begun joint maritime operations against boats supplying traffickers. The trip's earlier stops in Bogotá and Quito focused on the same theme with Presidents Abelardo de la Espriella and Daniel Noboa.

SourceInfobae (New York Times) / Moncloa

Texas — Nacogdoches County traffic stop turns up 100 pounds of cocaine, three arrested

45 kg cocaineNacogdoches County, Texas3 arrested

Nacogdoches County sheriff's deputies, aided by a newly certified K-9, stopped a vehicle on September 10 and found roughly 100 pounds of cocaine, about 45 kilograms, inside. Andre Nelson, 65, of Georgia; Antonio Colon, 46, of Illinois; and Latrisha Crims, 32, of Humble, Texas, were each charged with first-degree delivery of a controlled substance.

3South America

Colombia and the Netherlands intercept 297 kg of cocaine off La Guajira, four Venezuelans captured

297 kg cocaineLa Guajira, Colombia4 arrested

Colombia's Navy, working with a Dutch military patrol aircraft and the National Police, intercepted a go-fast boat in Caribbean waters off La Guajira department on September 10 and seized 297 kilograms of cocaine hydrochloride valued at more than $13 million. Four Venezuelan nationals aboard the vessel were captured.

Source724 Noticias

Ecuador seizes 200 kg of cocaine hidden in a banana shipment bound for Morocco

200 kg cocainePosorja, Ecuador

Ecuadorian police inspecting a truck on the road between Guayaquil and the port of Posorja on September 10 found 200 packages of cocaine hydrochloride, weighing about 200 kilograms, hidden in eight suitcases stashed among an export banana shipment. The cocaine, valued at roughly $6 million, was headed for the port of Tangier, Morocco.

SourceExtra.ec

Colombia's Navy and Brazil's Federal Police seize 4.5 tonnes of marijuana and machine guns on the Amazon River

4.5 t marijuanaCodajás, Amazonas, Brazil

Acting on a Colombian intelligence tip, Colombia's Navy and Air Force worked with Brazil's Federal Police to intercept a speedboat fitted with six outboard motors near Codajás, on the Amazon River in Brazilian territory, on September 10. Investigators believe the boat originated in the tri-border area shared by Colombia, Brazil and Peru; it was carrying 4.5 tonnes of high-potency marijuana along with two M60 machine guns, three AK-47 rifles and ammunition. Authorities valued the haul at more than $47 million and reported no arrests.

SourceInfobae

Argentina's Gendarmería seizes cocaine, cannabis and coca leaf in four Salta and Jujuy operations in a single day

128 kg cocaine, 117 kg cannabisSalta and Jujuy, Argentina4 arrested

Gendarmería Nacional ran four stops on September 10. On Route 34 in Jujuy province, agents found about 66 kilograms of cocaine during a vehicle stop near La Chaqueta and another 117 kilograms of cannabis in a package check near Chalicán, arresting two people. On Route 50 near Orán, Salta, gendarmes seized roughly 84 kilograms of coca leaf from a vehicle whose occupants were detained after the driver injured an officer, and separately found 62 kilograms of cocaine abandoned by three men who fled on foot near Río Pescado.

SourceArgentina.gob.ar

4Rest of World

French customs seize nearly 3.9 tonnes of cocaine hidden in plastic waste at the port of Dunkirk

3.9 t cocaineDunkirk, France

French customs officers opened a shipping container that had arrived from Ghana at the port of Dunkirk on September 7 and found 167 bales of cocaine, weighing close to 3.9 tonnes, concealed inside plastic waste; the container was booked to continue on to Antwerp. Officials valued the seizure, one of the largest ever made at a French port, at about €225 million and destroyed the cocaine. France's national anti-organized-crime prosecutor's office opened an investigation, but no arrests were reported.

SourceCNews / French Customs

India — Mizoram police seize meth and heroin in four operations near the Myanmar border, six held

17.6 kg methamphetamineMizoram, India6 arrested

Police and excise-and-narcotics officers ran four operations around the Saitual, Champhai and Kolasib districts of Mizoram on September 10, seizing a combined 17.6 kilograms of methamphetamine tablets and 141 grams of heroin worth more than 14 crore rupees (roughly $1.7 million). Six people were arrested, including five Myanmar nationals.

SourcePunjab Kesari

Europol dismantles the 'Dubai Bank' hawala network that laundered European drug profits

Spain / United Arab Emirates21 arrested

Spain's National Police, backed by Europol, announced on September 8 the results of a long-running investigation into 'Dubai Bank,' an underground hawala-style money-transfer network that moved criminal cash — much of it drug-trafficking proceeds — across borders without the funds ever touching the formal banking system. The operation has produced 21 arrests, including four in the United Arab Emirates, one in Egypt and one in the Netherlands, and authorities have seized or frozen about €20 million in assets, among them 48 properties and 121 bank accounts.

SourceEuropol

Mexico's Guerrero meth-lab campaign reaches Atoyac with another 166 kg seized

166 kg methamphetamineAtoyac de Álvarez, Guerrero, Mexico

Days after the Mexican Navy dismantled three clandestine labs in Zirándaro and Coahuayutla, the Army destroyed another methamphetamine lab on September 9 in the El Refugio community of Atoyac de Álvarez, in Guerrero's Costa Grande region, seizing about 166 kilograms of methamphetamine, 200 liters of precursor chemicals and nine synthesis reactors. Authorities estimated the hit to trafficking organizations at more than 3.1 billion pesos.

SourceQuadratín Guerrero
