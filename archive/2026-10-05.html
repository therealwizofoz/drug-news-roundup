1National / Federal (US)

US boat strike in the Caribbean kills four, taking the campaign to about 70 strikes and 235 dead

4 killedCaribbean Sea

SOUTHCOM said Joint Task Force Western Hemisphere hit a go-fast boat in the Caribbean on Oct. 4, killing four; no evidence of drugs aboard or the victims' identities was released. It was the first strike since the UN's Ben Saul said on Sept. 20 there were reasonable grounds to see the campaign as extrajudicial killings. CBS counts at least 235 dead in 70 strikes since September 2025, while UPI tallies 71 strikes and 238 dead, and the ACLU is pursuing a case alleging some victims were fishermen and farmers.

SourceCBS News / UPI / SOUTHCOM

Treasury sanctions 21 people and 25 entities in the Sinaloa Cartel's Los Mayos faction

21 people, 25 entitiesSinaloa, Mexico

OFAC announced the designations on Oct. 1 against the Los Mayos wing of the Sinaloa Cartel, covering alleged fentanyl traffickers, money launderers and facilitators who relied on corrupt local officials. The listings freeze US assets and bar Americans from dealing with those named.

SourceUS Treasury / The Yucatan Times

Two Dominican drug agents indicted in Miami after a DEA sting that lured them to Florida

5 kg+ cocaine chargedMiami, Florida2 charged

A Southern District of Florida grand jury charged DNCD officers Deny Sepúlveda de León and Oliver Guevara Rodríguez on Oct. 1 with conspiring to distribute and attempting to distribute five kilograms or more of cocaine for import into the US. They were arrested Sept. 28 after Dominican media said they were invited to Miami for a training course.

SourceRio Times

Charlotte-area task force indicts two Mexican nationals over 60 kilos of meth and fentanyl

~60 kg meth and fentanylCharlotte, North Carolina2 indicted

Martin Hernandez Nunez, 36, and Juan Carlos Mojarro Sanchez, 38, were indicted in the Western District of North Carolina after a Homeland Security Task Force probe that ended in May. Agents recovered about 60 kg of crystal meth and fentanyl in total, plus over $66,000 in alleged proceeds; the announcement came Sept. 23.

SourceUS Attorney's Office, W.D.N.C.

2Local US Arrests

Alhambra police K-9 finds about 200 kilos of cocaine in boxes inside an SUV

~200 kg cocaineAlhambra, California1 arrested

Alhambra, California officers stopped an SUV on Oct. 4 on Valley Boulevard near the 710 Freeway, and a dog alerted on cardboard boxes holding roughly 440 pounds (about 200 kg) of cocaine. Driver Julio Rangel of Huntington Park was arrested after giving shifting explanations for the cargo.

SourceFox News / Alhambra Police

3South America

Colombian police dismantle an ELN-linked cocaine lab near Cúcuta with 1.1 tonnes of product

1,120 kg cocaine, 125 kg baseCúcuta, Colombia8 arrested

A police-army operation on Oct. 3 in rural Cúcuta, Norte de Santander, hit a lab tied to the ELN's Frente Urbano Reinaldo Ardila, seizing 1,120 kg of cocaine and 125 kg of base paste plus chemicals. Eight suspects were arrested, including an alleged operational leader; the lab could reportedly make two tonnes a month.

SourceTDI Colombia

Colombian Navy stops a river boat carrying 1.5 tonnes of cocaine on the Meta toward Venezuela

1,498 kg cocaineRío Meta, Colombia

On Sept. 26 the Navy intercepted a boat on the Río Meta near the Venezuelan border carrying 1,498 kg of cocaine in 38 packages; two men aboard fled. Authorities said the load came from the La Primavera corridor and may have been meant for the ELN's eastern front.

SourceInfobae

Colombia captures the wife and daughter of Ecuador's Choneros boss Fito over a $25 million laundering case

$25M laundering caseSantander, Colombia2 arrested

Colombian and Ecuadorian authorities, with FBI help, arrested Inda Peñarrieta and Michelle Macías in Sabana de Torres, Santander on Sept. 27 under Interpol notices in Ecuador's "Blanqueo Fito" case. They were expelled and jailed at La Roca prison in Guayaquil on Sept. 29; neither is convicted.

SourceRio Times / InSight Crime

Ecuador finds 748 kilos of cocaine mixed into cement bound for Turkey at Posorja

748 kg cocainePosorja, Ecuador

Police Operation Fénix 0546 intercepted the cargo at the Posorja port terminal, announced Oct. 1. A separate Oct. 1 Posorja find turned up 37 packages of cocaine in a container for Germany and led to the detention of the exporter's representative.

SourceEl Diario (Ecuador) / Infobae

Brazilian highway police pull more than a tonne of drugs from trucks in Rondônia and Bahia

~1.15 t drugsRondônia and Bahia, Brazil

PRF officers on Oct. 4 stopped a truck on BR-364 in Presidente Médici, Rondônia carrying about 625 kg of drugs including 107.7 kg of cocaine and 47.7 kg of base paste. On Oct. 5 another stop on BR-116 in Vitória da Conquista, Bahia yielded about 522 kg, including 78 kg of cocaine and 10.5 kg of paste.

SourceBlog do Léo Santos / Jornal Info Rondônia

4Rest of World

Spanish customs find 862 kilos of cocaine in an Ecuadorian sugar container at Valencia

862 kg cocaineValencia, Spain

Officers selected the container on Sept. 27 and found 862 one-kilo bricks in 32 net-wrapped bundles stacked on the sugar, which had come from Guayaquil. Investigators suspect the load was hoisted aboard at sea with help from crew; no arrests were reported.

SourceThe Olive Press

Belgium, France and the Netherlands arrest 21 in a ring that moved cocaine on commercial flights

85 kg cocaine, 30 kg cannabisBelgium / France / Netherlands21 arrested

A Eurojust-coordinated action day on Sept. 30 saw 27 searches and 21 arrests, plus over €57,000 in cash, crypto wallets, eight vehicles and two guns. The case began with 30 kg of cocaine found in bags at Brussels Airport, later joined by a 55 kg cocaine and 30 kg cannabis seizure.

SourceEurojust

Dominican forces seize 749 packages from two go-fast boats off Baní, with DEA support

749 packagesBaní, Dominican Republic4 detained

The DNCD and Navy intercepted two boats off Baní on Oct. 4, one with 281 packages and one with 468, and detained four Dominicans; weights await forensic testing. SOUTHCOM-linked agencies and the DEA assisted, and a monkey was found aboard one boat.

SourceNoticias SIN / Infobae

Panama's Senan stops a speedboat with 2,852 packages south of Punta Burica

2,852 packagesChiriquí, Panama3 arrested

On Oct. 4 under Operation Protective Shield, the boat rammed a patrol vessel before being stopped and began taking on water. Three crew were arrested; Senan has seized 57,334 packages in 87 operations this year.

SourceNewsroom Panama
