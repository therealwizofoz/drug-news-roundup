New York State Police recover about 5.5 kg of suspected fentanyl after a night-time chase from East Fishkill to Putnam County

5.5 kg suspected fentanylPutnam County, New York1 arrested

Troopers tried to stop a vehicle on the Taconic State Parkway around 10:48 p.m. on October 19; the chase ran onto Interstate 84 and ended in Southeast, Putnam County. The lone occupant, a 36-year-old Brooklyn man, was charged with first- and third-degree drug possession and unlawful fleeing; the powder is suspected fentanyl pending lab results.

SourceUtica Phoenix

3South America

Colombian Navy seizes 1.6 tonnes of cocaine on a Chocó beach, tied by the president to the Clan del Golfo

1.65 t cocaineJuradó, Chocó, Colombia

The Pacific naval force, backed by naval intelligence, recovered 1,645 kilos of cocaine at Playa Octavia in Juradó, Chocó. President Abelardo De la Espriella linked the load to a Clan del Golfo substructure; no arrests were reported and the exact seizure date was not given.

SourceEl Heraldo

4Rest of World

Nicaraguan army intercepts about half a tonne of cocaine in sacks just off Managua's coast

~0.5 t cocaineVilla El Carmen, Nicaragua1 arrested

Naval forces and national police, under Operation Dignidad Nacional, stopped a boat roughly 100 metres off Playa Salamina in Villa El Carmen, finding 18 sacks holding 450 packages and 49 bulk bricks. One 59-year-old suspect was captured while two crew members jumped overboard and escaped; the operation was reported as taking place on October 20.

SourcePrensa Latina

Gujarat crime branches seize heroin and mephedrone worth about Rs 25 crore on the Ahmedabad-Vadodara Expressway

4.5 kg heroin, 2.5 kg mephedroneAhmedabad, Gujarat, India3 arrested

Joint Surat and Ahmedabad crime-branch teams stopped suspects near the CTM toll plaza, recovering 4.53 kg of heroin and 2.49 kg of mephedrone. Three men from New Delhi and Meerut were arrested; police say they belong to an inter-state network. The raid is believed to have happened on October 21.

SourceThe Tribune
