Texas — DEA and state troopers pull 82 kilos of cocaine and $700,000 from a Mission stash house

82 kg cocaineMission, Texas2 arrested

DEA's McAllen office and Texas Department of Public Safety agents raided a house in Mission around 8:50 p.m. on Sunday, August 30, finding 72 foil-and-plastic-wrapped cocaine bricks weighing about 82 kilograms hidden under a bed. A search of the kitchen freezer and master bedroom turned up more than $700,000 in cash bundled into two dozen packages. Two people were arrested.

SourceKVEO

2Local US Arrests

Pennsylvania — wiretaps unravel a Montgomery County cocaine and marijuana ring, ten charged

113.4 kg cannabis (250 lb)Montgomery County, Pennsylvania7 arrested, 3 sought

Montgomery County prosecutors said a months-long wiretap investigation traced a drug-trafficking operation running from Norristown into Chester and Delaware counties, including a storage facility in Devon used to process bulk marijuana. The case, announced September 1, produced about 250 pounds of marijuana, roughly $110,000 in cash and a dozen firearms. Ten people face charges; seven have been arrested, and three others — including alleged leader Sean Scott, held separately in Florida awaiting extradition — remain sought.

Source6ABC Philadelphia

3South America

Brazil — driver held after 4 tonnes of high-THC "skunk" cannabis found in a truck's hidden compartment

4,086.9 kg cannabisCristalina, Goiás, Brazil1 arrested

Federal Highway Police stopped a truck at a checkpoint on BR-040 in Cristalina, Goiás, on August 29 and found a concealed compartment packed with 3,420 blocks of plant material, weighing an estimated 4,086.9 kilograms. Prosecutors converted the driver's arrest into pretrial detention the next day; he told investigators he had loaded the skunk marijuana in the Federal District for a R$17,000 delivery fee to Belo Horizonte. Police called it the largest skunk seizure the highway patrol has made in the capital region this year.

SourceJornal de Brasília

Brazil — bus passenger caught with 26 kilos of marijuana and a rifle magazine on a Bahia highway

26 kg cannabisFeira de Santana, Bahia, Brazil1 arrested

Federal Highway Police stopped an interstate bus at kilometer 429 of BR-116 in Feira de Santana, Bahia, on the morning of September 1 and found a woman traveling from Rio de Janeiro with about a kilogram of marijuana in a backpack. A further search of the bus's lower luggage hold turned up another 25 kilograms, along with a rifle magazine and 37 rounds of ammunition. She was headed to Capela do Alto Alegre and was booked at the Sobradinho police complex.

SourceInterior da Bahia

Argentina — Mendoza police seize 34 kilos of cocaine and $18,200 in a Guaymallén raid

34 kg cocaineGuaymallén, Mendoza, Argentina3 arrested

Anti-narcotics police in Guaymallén, Mendoza province, intercepted nearly 34 kilograms of high-purity cocaine wrapped in green-and-yellow tape on September 2, along with $18,200 in cash; three people were detained and the case went to federal court to trace the shipment's origin and destination. Alongside it, a separate Federal Police investigation carried out twelve simultaneous raids in Las Heras and Guaymallén, arresting five more people accused of running a local distribution network.

SourceKCH FM

4Rest of World

Australia — Canberra commits $600 million to choke Pacific drug-smuggling routes

Australia / Pacific Islands

Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced an AU$600 million "Waqa Moana" initiative at the Pacific Islands Forum in Palau on September 2, funding ocean surveillance, interception of smuggling boats and narco-submarines, and closer police coordination across Pacific nations alongside cooperation with South and Central American authorities. Officials pointed to more than 17 tonnes of cocaine and methamphetamine seized in Australia so far this year, up from 5.5 tonnes in all of 2025, as evidence traffickers are increasingly routing shipments through the Pacific toward Sydney, Brisbane and Melbourne.

SourceABC News (Australia)

Nigeria — Kaduna anti-drug command reports 140 arrests and 816 kilos seized in a month of raids

Kaduna State, Nigeria140 arrested

The NDLEA's Kaduna State Command said operations across the state in August led to 140 arrests and the seizure of roughly 816.5 kilograms of drugs, most of it cannabis, with smaller amounts of cocaine, tramadol, methamphetamine and other substances. Commander Maijama'a Muhammad said agents also shut down 107 suspected drug dens and recovered ammunition and a locally made pistol during the sweep.

SourceBusinessDay Nigeria

5Ongoing Stories with New Developments

Sri Lanka meth bust — DEA reveals it was a joint operation with a bigger haul than first reported

Colombo, Sri Lanka5 arrested

A day after Colombo Port authorities announced a 463-kilogram methamphetamine seizure, the US DEA and Sri Lanka's Police Narcotics Bureau disclosed on September 2 that the bust was a joint operation, built on tracking shipping containers from Pakistan between August 14 and 29. Updated figures put the haul at about 471 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine, valued near $21 million and hidden in bath towels, with five people now under arrest — three Pakistani nationals and two Sri Lankans — up from the two Pakistani nationals reported the day before.

SourceEuronews
